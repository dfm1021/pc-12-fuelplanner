Features

Built around how
PC-12 pilots actually work.

Not a generic fuel calculator. Every feature comes from real Part 135 operations.

```
⚖️
Weight-Based Planning
All fuel in pounds at 6.75 lbs/gal. MGTOW enforcement at every departure — 10,450 lbs for standard NG, 9,921 lbs for lighter variants.
🗺️
Multi-Leg Trip Planning
Plan the full trip in one shot. Departure fuel calculated using a backward pass that accounts for every planned fuel stop along the route.
⛽
FBO Cost Optimization
Automatic take-fuel-or-tanker decision at every stop. Formula: (dest price − home price) × waiver gallons vs. handling fee.
🔒
Contingency Enforcement
600 or 700 lb contingency is the absolute floor at every landing — intermediate stops included. The model cannot plan a landing below it.
📋
Discretionary Fuel Log
Pilots can add fuel above the computed load, but a written justification is required. Every entry is logged with pilot name and timestamp.
🧮
Fuel Order Calculator
Enter fuel on board in pounds. Get exact gallons to order and gallons per wing — so pilots can hand the FBO a single number on arrival.
✈️
Custom Fleet Profiles
Upload your own fleet of PC-12s with individual tail numbers, empty weights, and MGTOW data. Every aircraft in your operation, configured exactly right.
```
